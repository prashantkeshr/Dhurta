Privacy Policy

Dhurta is built on a single principle: your browsing is yours alone.

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Applies to Dhurta Browser v1.0 and later

Summary: Dhurta Browser collects no personal data, sends no telemetry, and stores everything locally on your device. We have nothing to sell or share.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Dhurta Browser does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Browsing history or visited URLs
  • Search queries
  • Passwords, credentials, or form data
  • Device identifiers or IP addresses
  • Crash reports or diagnostic telemetry
  • Usage analytics or behavioural metrics
  • Location data

2. Local Storage Only

All data created while using Dhurta — bookmarks, history, settings, cookies, cached pages, and session data — is stored exclusively on your local device. Dhurta.inc has no access to this data, and it is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

3. Built-In Privacy Features

Dhurta includes several privacy-enhancing features by default:

  • Ad & tracker blocking — network-level blocking of known tracking domains
  • Fingerprint spoofing — randomises hardware and browser fingerprints to prevent cross-site tracking
  • WebRTC leak protection — prevents exposure of your real IP address through WebRTC
  • Ghost Mode — enhanced private browsing that leaves no local trace
  • Tor routing — optional anonymous routing through the Tor network
  • HTTPS enforcement — upgrades connections to HTTPS where available

4. Network Requests

When you browse the web, your browser communicates with the websites you visit. This is inherent to how the internet works. Dhurta does not intercept, log, or forward these communications. The content of your browsing sessions is never seen by Dhurta.inc.

Dhurta may check for software updates by contacting our update server. This check includes your current version number and operating system, but no personally identifiable information.

5. Third-Party Services

Dhurta does not bundle or enable any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. When you use a website or web service through Dhurta, that website's own privacy policy applies to your interaction with it.

6. Children's Privacy

Dhurta Browser is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because Dhurta does not collect any personal data at all, this standard is met by default.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of Dhurta after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
