Our Manifesto
Your browser is the most intimate software on your machine. It knows every site you visit, every search you make, every password you type. It should answer to you alone.
01 — SOVEREIGNTY
Your data, your machine

Every byte Dhurta processes stays on your hardware. We collect nothing — no crash reports, no usage analytics, no telemetry of any kind.

02 — TRANSPARENCY
Open source, open trust

Every line of Dhurta is public on GitHub. Privacy claims that can't be verified are marketing. We built the receipts in.

03 — AUTONOMY
No accounts. No subscriptions.

Download and run. No sign-up, no email, no license key, no paywall. Privacy is the baseline every person deserves by default.
